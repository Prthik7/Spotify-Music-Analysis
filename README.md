## 🎵 Spotify Listening Analytics Dashboard

### 📊 Overview

This Power BI dashboard provides an in-depth analysis of a user’s Spotify listening behavior over the years. It visualizes data such as listening patterns by hour, track frequency, artist/album trends, device usage, and more.

---

### 🔍 Key Insights

#### ⏰ **Listening Pattern by Hour & Day**

* **Peak Hours**: Highest listening observed at **8 PM to 11 PM**.
* **Busiest Day**: **Saturday** shows the most activity, with over 1,400 listens during peak hours.
* **Least Active Hour**: Between **8 AM to 12 PM**, with a significant drop.

#### 🎧 **Album & Artist Trends**

* **Albums Played**: 7,905 across years.
* **Top 5 Albums**:
